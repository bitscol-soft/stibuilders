The idea

The Bandon series is what a good small footprint can do. Six seven-storey buildings within two streets of each other and of the first metro station, each holding three 1,750 sq ft flats per floor.

All six completed and handed over between 2022 and 2024. Owners here get the advantage of a shared maintenance team across the cluster without the service charge of a gated compound.
