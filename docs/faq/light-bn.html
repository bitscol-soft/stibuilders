Help centre

Questions we get asked most

Buying, booking, land sharing, payments and handover — answered plainly. If yours is not here, call the hotline.

Buying

Choose a project, select an available unit and submit a reservation through the booking page. The booking amount is refundable for seven days. Our desk then prepares the buyer agreement, payment schedule and registration papers.

The quoted price covers the apartment as per the finishing specification. Registration, utility connections, car parking (where charged separately) and service charges for the first year are additional and listed in the payment plan.

Yes. We work with most scheduled banks in Bangladesh and can share the approved project documents required for a mortgage. Our documentation desk assists with the bank file at no extra cost.

Ten years on the structural frame, five years on waterproofing and two years on finishing. The warranty is written into the buyer agreement, not just the brochure.

Booking

Yes — site visits are arranged every Saturday and Wednesday. You will be given a safety briefing, a helmet and a walkthrough with the site engineer.

The agreement includes a delay clause: we pay a monthly compensation to the buyer for every month beyond the committed handover date, except where the delay is caused by force majeure or a change requested by the buyer.

Land sharing

STI Builders funds RAJUK approval, design, construction and utility connections. The land owner contributes the land and does not need to raise capital.

The split depends on plot size, road width, allowable FAR and the condition of the title. Typical agreements range from 50:50 to 55:45 on net sellable area, fixed in the deed before construction begins.

The land owner’s share is handed over first at completion — this is written into the deed. Registration and mutation costs for the owner’s units are borne by us.

You may sell at any time. We offer a first right of refusal and can list the units through our own sales desk.
