Land owner FAQ

The questions land owners ask us

STI Builders funds RAJUK approval, design, construction and utility connections. The land owner contributes the land and does not need to raise capital.

The split depends on plot size, road width, allowable FAR and the condition of the title. Typical agreements range from 50:50 to 55:45 on net sellable area, fixed in the deed before construction begins.

The land owner’s share is handed over first at completion — this is written into the deed. Registration and mutation costs for the owner’s units are borne by us.

You may sell at any time. We offer a first right of refusal and can list the units through our own sales desk.
