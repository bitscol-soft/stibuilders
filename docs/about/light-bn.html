Our story

Fourteen years,
one standard.

Building considered addresses across Dhaka since 2012 — apartments, commercial floors and land-sharing developments delivered with structural integrity and quiet elegance.

Every project starts with the site. We walk the plot, study the light and the road, and only then draw. Buyers get the full drawing set, the approval papers and a named project adviser for the life of the building.

See our workTalk to us

Design first

In-house architects and structural engineers work on the same drawing set, so what is drawn is what is buildable.

Compliance, not shortcuts

RAJUK approval, BNBC 2020 seismic detailing and environment clearance on every project — paper on file before piling.

One adviser, end to end

The person who shows you the site is the person who answers your call during construction and after handover.

Handover on schedule

Milestone reporting every month, and a handover date written into the agreement — not just the brochure.

Build and report
